01 / THE VISION

Beyond seeing.
Toward understanding.

Video is one of the richest forms of perceptual data, yet reasoning about events across time remains a fundamental challenge. Systems must do more than recognize what is present: they must infer why events happen, how they relate, and what happens next.

This workshop brings together researchers working on video understanding and generation, spanning both fully observed and streaming settings. We aim to connect advances in multimodal learning with the demands of robotics, embodied agents, and other real-world applications.

Streaming & online video

Partial observability, incremental inference, belief updates, and real-time reasoning.

05

Datasets & evaluation

Benchmarks beyond final-answer accuracy, including trajectory-level and faithfulness metrics.

06

Grounding & simulation

Physical, causal, and intent grounding through interventions, simulation, and counterfactuals.

07

Efficient architectures

Memory, long-context modeling, and multimodal fusion for extended videos.

08

Reliability & applications

Failure analysis, hallucination, reasoning drift, robotics, embodied agents, and healthcare.
